## Session-hardening invariants (still hard for v1.12.0)

1. After an administrator password reset or account disable for a user, no new refresh token may be minted for that user.
2. A refresh token may be consumed at most once; replay of a consumed token revokes only that session family.
3. Unrelated users/devices keep valid sessions.
4. Access-JWT lifetime and established WebSocket teardown are out of scope unless separately implemented.
