### Session-hardening invariants (hard gate)

P1-A (admin credential/disable refresh revoke) and P1-B (atomic refresh rotation / reuse detection, including login mint safety) are part of this gate. v1.12.0 must not ship until these invariants are proven on real SQLite and independently reviewed:

1. After an administrator password reset or account disable for a user, no new refresh token may be minted for that user (covers both token rotation and login).
2. A refresh token may be consumed at most once; replay of a consumed token revokes only that session family.
3. Unrelated users/devices keep valid sessions; do not globally revoke all devices as the default fix.
4. Access-JWT lifetime and established WebSocket teardown are out of scope unless separately implemented and tested — do not claim them from P1-A/P1-B alone.

P1-A alone is **not** releasable for this gate. Merge/release only with P1-B (and login mint checks) as a hard dependency.
